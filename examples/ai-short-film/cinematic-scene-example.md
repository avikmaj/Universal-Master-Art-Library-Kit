# AI Short Film Example

## Objective

Create a cinematic image prompt for an emotional, story-driven AI short-film scene.

## Brief

- Genre: Science-fiction drama
- Scene purpose: Establish the protagonist's isolation before a turning point
- Subject: A solitary engineer in a compact futuristic observation room
- Composition: Medium-wide shot; character positioned on the left third; window dominates the right side
- Camera: 35 mm cinematic lens impression; eye-level viewpoint; shallow but readable depth
- Environment: Rain-streaked orbital city viewed through a large window
- Lighting: Cool blue exterior spill with a warm practical lamp on the character
- Palette: Deep teal, muted slate, restrained amber
- Style: Premium cinematic film still; realistic production design
- Delivery: 16:9, safe space at upper right for title overlay
- Avoid: Neon overload, unreadable screens, duplicate limbs, random text, cartoon proportions

## Model-neutral prompt

Create a 16:9 premium cinematic film still for a science-fiction drama. A solitary engineer sits at a compact workstation in a futuristic observation room, positioned on the left third of the frame. A rain-streaked panoramic window occupies the right side, revealing a distant orbital city. Use an eye-level medium-wide composition with a 35 mm cinematic lens character, restrained depth of field, cool blue exterior light, and one warm practical desk lamp shaping the engineer's face. Grade in deep teal, muted slate, and restrained amber. Leave clean negative space in the upper-right area for a title overlay. Maintain realistic production design and coherent physical scale. Avoid neon overload, pseudo-text on screens, distorted anatomy, duplicate limbs, and cartoon styling.
