# Universal Image Prompt Framework

## Core visual brief

Use this structure before converting into a platform-specific image prompt.

- Objective: What must this image accomplish?
- Sector: Which image-production domain applies?
- Subject: Who or what is the primary focus?
- Audience: Who will see or use the image?
- Output channel: Thumbnail, ad, poster, ecommerce listing, UI, presentation, film frame, or another format?
- Composition: Subject placement, camera distance, perspective, frame balance, and negative space.
- Camera: Lens character, shot type, angle, depth of field, and viewpoint.
- Lighting: Direction, softness, contrast, time of day, practical lights, and mood.
- Color: Palette, contrast, saturation, brand colors, and grading direction.
- Style: Photorealism, illustration, 3D, animation, editorial, painting, or hybrid style.
- Materials and detail: Important surfaces, textures, clothing, product details, and environmental cues.
- Constraints: Facts that must remain accurate and elements that must not appear.
- Reference inputs: Source images and what must be preserved from each.
- Delivery: Aspect ratio, resolution, crop safety, text-safe areas, and background requirements.

## Universal template

Create a [image type] for [audience and use case].

Primary subject: [subject].
Primary objective: [objective].
Composition: [composition].
Camera and framing: [camera].
Environment: [setting].
Lighting: [lighting].
Palette and color grade: [color direction].
Visual medium: [style].
Important details: [details that must be accurate].
Output: [aspect ratio and delivery requirements].

Constraints:
- Preserve: [non-negotiable details].
- Exclude: [unwanted elements].
- Avoid: distorted anatomy, malformed objects, duplicate details, unintended text, unreadable labels, and inconsistent lighting.
