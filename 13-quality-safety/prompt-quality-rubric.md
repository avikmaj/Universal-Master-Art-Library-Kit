# Quality Review Rubric

Review every final prompt and every generated image against these questions.

## Intent

- Does the image clearly serve the stated objective?
- Is the primary focal point immediately clear?
- Is the audience and output channel reflected in the design?

## Fidelity

- Are all non-negotiable details preserved?
- If using reference images, did the result preserve the correct subject identity, product geometry, or brand attributes?
- Are physical scale, perspective, anatomy, and object count coherent?

## Composition

- Does the crop suit the delivery format?
- Is there usable safe space for text where required?
- Is the visual hierarchy intentional?

## Technical quality

- Are there malformed hands, faces, repeated objects, artifacts, false labels, or nonsensical text?
- Does lighting agree with shadows and the environment?
- Are materials and surfaces plausible?

## Rights and safety

- Do not imply unverified brand claims.
- Do not use a person's likeness without an appropriate right or authorization.
- Do not imitate a living artist as a replacement for a distinct original visual direction.
- Use culturally and religiously sensitive subjects respectfully.
