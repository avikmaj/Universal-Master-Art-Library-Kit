# Claude Visual Brief Adapter

Claude may be used as a planning, critique, and prompt-authoring layer.

## Task

Read the selected sector context and core-control files. Produce:

1. A structured visual brief
2. A final model-neutral image prompt
3. A constraints list
4. A quality-review checklist
5. Optional conversion notes for another image generator

## Principle

Do not invent brand facts, product specifications, identity traits, or reference-image details. Ask for them when they materially affect fidelity.
