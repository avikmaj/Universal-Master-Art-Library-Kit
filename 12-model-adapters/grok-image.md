# Grok Image Adapter

Convert the visual brief into a direct image-generation request.

## Required elements

- Subject and use case
- Composition and framing
- Environment and lighting
- Visual style and color direction
- Aspect ratio or platform destination
- Non-negotiable preservation requirements
- Exclusions and quality constraints

## Output format

Return:

1. Final image prompt
2. Preserve exactly
3. Avoid
4. Optional variation directions
