# Semiconductor and Engineering Visuals

## Purpose

Create technically credible imagery for semiconductor design verification, silicon, FPGA, SoC architecture, engineering presentations, product communications, lab environments, and developer education.

## Ask before prompting

- Is this a conceptual, educational, product-marketing, or technically literal image?
- Which specific technology or abstraction level is needed: transistor, die, package, board, FPGA, SoC, testbench, waveform, lab, server, or data center?
- Must labels, interfaces, ports, package geometry, or diagrams be technically correct?
- Is the desired visual type a schematic, infographic, isometric render, cinematic hero image, or presentation illustration?
- Which audience is targeted: engineers, executives, students, customers, or the general public?

## Universal prompt template

Create a [visual type] illustrating [technology or engineering concept] for [audience].

Focus on [primary technical story].
Show [required components] with clear hierarchy.
Use [diagrammatic, photorealistic, cinematic, isometric, or editorial] visual language.
Composition: [composition].
Lighting and color: [lighting and palette].
Delivery: [presentation, technical article, social post, product page, or other output], [aspect ratio].

Preserve technical credibility for [non-negotiable items].
Avoid pseudo-text, impossible connectors, random labels, malformed chips, and misleading technical relationships.

## Quality checklist

- The conceptual hierarchy is immediately clear
- The desired technical abstraction level is consistent
- No fake specifications are implied
- Diagrams do not suggest false implementation details
- Brand or product claims are supplied by the user, not invented
